Why a stale press page costs you

It is a credibility check

Prospects, partners and journalists open the press page to see whether a company is active. A page whose newest item is from 2023 answers that question badly.

Newsroom questions

What is a company newsroom page?

A section of your website that lists your announcements in reverse order, each on its own permanent URL with the right markup. It is where journalists, customers and search systems look for your official statements, and it is one of the few pages on a business site that improves simply by existing and staying current.

Is the newsroom really free?

Yes, with any plan including a single $99 release. You get a hosted newsroom on our domain and an embeddable widget for your own site. It keeps working whether or not you buy again, which is deliberate.

How do I add the newsroom to my own website?

One script tag wherever you want the releases to appear. It works on WordPress, Wix, Squarespace, Shopify, Webflow or hand-written HTML, and it does not require a plugin or a developer.

Does the embed affect my SEO?

The embed renders in the browser, so it does not pass or receive ranking credit in either direction and it is not a link scheme. Its value is that your press page stays current without anyone maintaining it, and that your visitors and any human reviewing your business see an active company.

Can publishers embed our releases on their sites?

Yes, and it is free for them. A site owner can embed the feed filtered to their industry or region and get a stream of relevant news with correct attribution. That is how our customers earn genuine placements rather than purchased ones.
