About the builder

      

      

        What is the standard press release format?
        
Headline, dateline, opening paragraph containing the complete news, two or three paragraphs of context, one attributed quote, a boilerplate About section and a media contact. Between 400 and 600 words. This builder assembles exactly that from what you type.

      

      

        Does this tool write the release for me?
        
No. It formats what you supply into the correct structure. It does not invent facts, write your quote or produce persuasive copy, because a tool that guessed at your facts would be worse than useless. If you want it written, that is included in the $99 service.

      

      

        Can I download the result?
        
Yes, as a plain text file, or you can copy it. Plain text is the right format to send — PDFs and designed layouts make the content harder for both editors and automated systems to use.

      

      

        Where do I send it once it is written?
        
Publish it on your own site at a permanent URL first, then distribute it. Our guide on how to distribute a press release covers the full technical process if you want to run it yourself.
