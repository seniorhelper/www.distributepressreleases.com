Madwackd Productions, the Denver studio behind the AI video platform Madwackd Studio, has released the official trailer for THE EARTH MOVIE, its first feature film: a family-friendly satire made with AI-generated characters, voices and imagery. The four-minute trailer is out now at [theearthmovie.com](https://www.theearthmovie.com/#trailer). Presale tickets are on sale now for $2.99, against a regular price of $4.99 at release, with a full refund available on request any time before the film premieres.

THE EARTH MOVIE is set on a backwards planet hooked on its feeds, where everyone keeps score with a glowing number over their head. One scrawny nobody wakes up and starts asking the question nobody above the clouds wants asked, and learns that love and kindness beat money. The jokes stay clean, with no cussing and no crude humor, while the satire carries allegories that older viewers will catch. The film is targeted for Summer 2027 at [watchvrmovie.com](https://watchvrmovie.com/), an online theater viewers can visit with a VR headset or on a phone, tablet or computer. There is no fixed premiere date yet.

> We made a movie about a planet that forgot what matters, and we wanted the tickets to remember. Every early ticket helps us finish the film, and a piece of every ticket goes to pulling carbon back out of the sky.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Madwackd Productions.

## Presale tickets

Early ticket sales help pay to finish making the film. One $2.99 presale ticket lets one person watch THE EARTH MOVIE online at watchvrmovie.com when it premieres; access details are emailed before premiere day. Buyers can ask for a full refund at any time before release, with no reason needed, and the studio has committed in its published terms to premiere or contact every buyer by September 30, 2027. A ticket is a ticket to watch a movie: it is not an investment, a donation or a share in the film. The free waitlist gets the same news for anyone who would rather wait. Full terms are at [theearthmovie.com/tickets/](https://www.theearthmovie.com/tickets/).

## 1.5% to carbon removal

Because the film is about a planet that forgot what matters, 1.5% of all ticket sales goes to removing carbon dioxide from the air through [Stripe Climate](https://stripe.com/climate), which routes funds to permanent carbon removal projects. The contribution comes out of the studio's share, at no extra cost to ticket buyers.
