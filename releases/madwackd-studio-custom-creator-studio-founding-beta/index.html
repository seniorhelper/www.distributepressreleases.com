Madwackd Studio Opens the Custom Creator Studio Founding Beta at $99 a Month for the First 100 Creators

Published November 8, 2026 by Madwackd Studio

Synthetic narration. AI disclosure.

DENVER, Colo., November 8, 2026 — Madwackd Studio, the AI video studio from Denver's Madwackd Productions, today opened the Custom Creator Studio, a personalized creator dashboard built from one conversation, in a Founding Creator beta priced at $99 a month for the first 12 months. The founding rate is open to the first 100 founding creators or through November 22, 2026, whichever comes first, after which the plan renews at the regular price of $299 a month. The studio is live at madwackd.ai/custom-creator-studio.

A creator tells Genie, the studio's intake assistant run by its AI director Producer Z, what they make, where they post and what a good month looks like. The dashboard appears in minutes with the creator's brand on it and the studio's tools inside: scripts, hooks and captions, a voice and music mixer, a storyboard and timeline, and AI video renders priced before the creator confirms. A human producer then reviews the brand kit, tone and plan within one business day, and drafts one press release that the creator approves before it is published on the Madwackd distribution network. Creators cut, caption and export for TikTok, Reels, Shorts, YouTube, Instagram, X, LinkedIn and allofus.one from one panel and share with one tap; direct posting to each platform rolls out only after that platform approves the Madwackd app.

The Founding Creator plan includes every studio tool, the personalized dashboard and production team, the human producer pass, 120 Fast render credits a month, 3 Talking Z voice minutes, one published press release and a Creator Spotlight on madwackd.ai. Beta plan credits reset each month, and credit packs start at $14. The plan can be cancelled anytime in one click, carries a 14-day money-back guarantee on the first payment, and notifies subscribers 30 days before the month-13 price change. Creators own the projects they make, every project comes with a rights pack, and AI-generated clips carry an AI label.

Tell the Genie what you want your creator dashboard to do and look like. Poof. Done. A custom creator studio built around you, with an AI production team behind you, and a real producer who checks every line before it counts.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Madwackd Productions.

How to join

Creators can tell Genie what they want to make at madwackd.ai/custom-creator-studio and join the Founding Creator list. Nothing is charged on the page; a producer replies by email within one business day with the checkout link. The free Creator Studio tools remain open to everyone at no charge, and a plain-language explainer of what the plan includes is at madwackd.ai/producers-corner. Questions go to 1-800-481-8638.

About Madwackd Studio

Madwackd Studio is the studio layer on top of today's AI video models: it keeps characters and voices consistent, adds a voice, shows the price before any render runs, and ships finished video with a rights pack. It is built by Madwackd Productions, the Denver studio making comedy shorts, cartoons, ads and its first feature, EARTH, and founded by Zach Wennstedt. The studio is headquartered at 1001 Bannock St #660, Denver, Colorado, and makes video for businesses and creators worldwide.
