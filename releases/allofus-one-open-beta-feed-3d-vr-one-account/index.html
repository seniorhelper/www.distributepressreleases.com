allofus.one Opens Its Free Social Platform in Beta: One Account for a Chronological Feed, 3D Worlds and VR in the Browser

Published October 11, 2026 by allofus.one

Synthetic narration. AI disclosure.

DENVER, Colo., October 11, 2026 — allofus.one, a free social platform built in Denver, is now open to the public in beta at allofus.one. One account works three ways. There is a regular social feed. There is a 3D world that runs in a phone or laptop browser. And the same world opens in virtual reality on a Meta Quest headset through the headset’s web browser, with nothing to install.

The feed is chronological. Posts from the people and groups a member chooses appear in the order they were made, with no ranking algorithm deciding what to show first. Members can download their data or delete their account from the Control panel at any time. Sponsored posts in the feed are labeled, and members can switch them off in their ad settings.

One account, three ways in

  • Feed. Personal pages, connections, groups, reactions, comments, photo and video posts, polls and buy, sell and trade groups, at allofus.one.
  • 3D. A browser-based world at allofus.one/world/ with avatars, homes members can claim and build, live rooms, games and places to explore.
  • VR. The same world in WebXR on a Quest 3 headset, with controller movement, teleport, a laser pointer and a wrist menu.

Members switch between the three with the Feed, 3D and VR switcher at the top of the site, so a conversation that starts in the feed can move into the 3D world without a new sign-up.

“Your feed should show the people you chose, in the order they posted. We built that first, and then built a place to actually meet, on a phone, a laptop or a headset, with one account. It’s a beta, so we read every bug report and we want people to tell us what to fix,” allofus.one said in a statement.

Free tools built in

Every member gets the same set of free tools:

  • LIFEboard, a private planner with goals, habits, a journal, a focus timer, reminders that export to a phone calendar, and budgeting and debt-payoff calculators
  • Groups and Spaces, which give any group its own feed, members, events and courses, open or invite-only
  • Events with RSVPs, calendar files and a free video room for each event
  • Courses with ordered lessons, video links and a progress bar
  • Direct messages and video calls for up to six people in the browser, with no download and no account needed for guests
  • Brand pages, so a small business can run a page from a personal profile

Who it is for

allofus.one is aimed at people looking for an alternative to the big social networks who want to see what their friends post, along with community groups, clubs, coaches and small businesses that need events, courses and video calls without paying for separate software. Accounts are open to anyone 13 and older. The platform runs a language filter, report and block tools, and its voice chat keeps teens with teens and adults with adults.

Join the beta

The beta is free and open now. Sign up at https://allofus.one, or open allofus.one/world/ in the browser on a Quest headset to try the VR mode. Bug reports can be sent from the report button in the feed or from inside VR.

About allofus.one

allofus.one is a free social platform built in Denver. One account gives members a chronological feed, a 3D world in the browser and a VR mode for Quest headsets, plus free built-in tools including LIFEboard, groups, events, courses, direct messages and video calls. allofus.one is operated by Eye To Ad Media, headquartered in Denver, Colorado.
