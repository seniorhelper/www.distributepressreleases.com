# Eye To Ad Media Opens Total Market Domination, Selling One Exclusive Industry Slot Per State on Its Business Directory Network

Denver-based Eye To Ad Media has opened Total Market Domination, an advertising package that sells one exclusive slot per industry in each state across the business directory network the company owns. When someone searches the network for an industry and a location in that state, such as "dentist Orlando" or "Omaha auto repair," the business holding the slot appears in a pop-up on the results, and its large banners sit on the matching search and category pages.

The network covers USABusinessSearch.com nationwide and adds COBusinessList.com for Colorado. While a business holds a slot, no competitor in its industry can buy the same slot in that state.

## Why one slot per state

Exclusive directory sponsorships are often sold by a local newspaper or chamber of commerce, one town or one print edition at a time. Total Market Domination works at state level instead. That suits companies that serve a whole state or several metros in one: a roofer covering the Front Range, an HVAC company working across Arizona, or a personal injury firm that would rather claim a practice area statewide than fight for one city at a time.

> We built this for owners who win on call volume and are tired of sharing every placement with every competitor in town. One business gets the slot for its industry in its state. We're also plain about what it isn't: it's advertising on directories we own, not Google, and nobody should promise you a number of calls.

Eye To Ad Media, in a company statement.

## What is included

- An exclusive slot for one industry in one state on the network
- A pop-up placement on searches that match the industry and a location in that state
- Large banner placements on matching search and category pages
- The business name, phone number and website link on every placement
- USABusinessSearch.com nationwide, with COBusinessList.com added for Colorado slots
- Exact placements and terms confirmed in writing before the campaign starts

## Pricing and limits

The normal rate is $2,500 per industry, per month, per state. Eye To Ad Media is offering lower launch rates while the network opens, and businesses can ask for current launch pricing in their market. Reach depends on how many people search the network for that industry in that state, and the company says it talks through what to expect before anyone commits. Total Market Domination does not change Google's results or ads and does not guarantee rankings. For a business that mainly needs to rank higher on Google itself, Eye To Ad Media says it will recommend SEO work first.
