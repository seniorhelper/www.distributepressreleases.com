The feed matrix

One firehose is useless. Filtered feeds get subscribed to.

Most wires publish a single feed containing everything they have ever sent. Nobody subscribes to that, because a regional home services blog does not want biotech funding news.

Ours are split by industry and by region, so a publisher can take exactly the slice they cover and nothing else. That is the difference between a feed people keep and a feed people unsubscribe from in a week — and a subscribed feed is a distribution channel that keeps working after the first day.

Channel questions

Which press release distribution channels are open to anyone?

Search engine push protocols such as IndexNow, syndication feeds in RSS, Atom, JSON Feed and Media RSS, podcast directories that accept any valid feed, open social protocols including Bluesky and Mastodon, feed readers, and any publisher who chooses to subscribe to your feed or embed your content. Google News inclusion is open in the sense that it is algorithmic rather than an application, but it is not guaranteed.

Why can't a small service distribute to AP or Reuters?

Because that access is contractual rather than technical. The large wires hold syndication agreements with those organizations built over decades. There is no clever engineering that substitutes for the contract, and any small service claiming that reach should be asked to show you the placement.

Does Google support IndexNow?

No. Google has tested the protocol but has not adopted it. The participating engines are Bing, Yandex, Naver, Seznam and several smaller ones. We say this explicitly because several services imply otherwise.

How does Google News inclusion work now?

Manual publication submission closed in 2025 and inclusion is now algorithmic. What a publisher can do is meet the technical and editorial signals: a news sitemap covering the last 48 hours, NewsArticle and NewsMediaOrganization markup, author pages, a corrections policy and published editorial standards. We build to all of that. Inclusion remains Google's decision.

Can publishers republish my release?

Yes, and we make it easy on purpose. Our feeds are filtered by industry and region so a publisher can take only what is relevant, and there is a free embed any site owner can drop in. Republished copies carry canonical attribution back to the original.
