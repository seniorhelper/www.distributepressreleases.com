Press release examples small businesses can actually use

Eight common announcements, a sample headline and opening for each, and why it works.

What does a good small business press release look like?

It names the company in the headline, states the news with a specific number, place or date, and puts the complete story in the first paragraph. One quote from a named person adds a reason or judgment. A short boilerplate and a media contact close it. The examples below follow that pattern for the eight announcements small businesses make most often.

Published November 17, 2026. We date these pages because the rules in this field change and undated advice ages badly.

Every company and person on this page is fictional. The structure is the part to copy: swap in your own names, numbers and dates, and keep the order. If you want the format explained step by step first, read how to write a press release.

What every example below has in common

  • The headline leads with the company name and stays under about twelve words.
  • The opening sentence answers who, what, when and where, with at least one checkable number.
  • No adjective appears that a stranger could not verify.
  • The quote explains why, rather than repeating what.

1. A new location or expansion

Ridgeline Bakery Opens Second Shop in Golden, Adding 9 Jobs

GOLDEN, Colo., April 2, 2027 — Ridgeline Bakery opened its second retail shop at 1200 Washington Avenue on April 1, adding nine full-time jobs and extending weekday hours to 6 a.m. for commuters heading into the foothills.

Why it works: address, date, a job count and a concrete change for customers. Local editors care about jobs and addresses because their readers do.

2. A key hire or promotion

Harbor & Pine Accounting Names Dana Ruiz Director of Payroll Services

TACOMA, Wash., May 6, 2027 — Harbor & Pine Accounting has promoted Dana Ruiz to director of payroll services, where she will lead a team of six serving more than 300 small business clients in Pierce County.

Why it works: full name, exact title, team size, client count and area. Business journals often run short people-on-the-move items built from exactly these facts. Include a headshot and a one-line career summary.

3. A new product or service

Summit Lawn Care Launches Battery-Powered Mowing Service in Boise

BOISE, Idaho, March 10, 2027 — Summit Lawn Care will begin offering residential mowing with an all-battery equipment fleet on March 15, serving homes within 15 miles of downtown Boise at the same weekly rate as its existing service.

Why it works: it says what is new, when it starts, where it is available and what it costs relative to before. "Innovative eco-friendly solution" would have told the reader nothing.

4. An award, certification or license

Clearwater Plumbing Earns State Master Plumber License for Fifth Technician

TAMPA, Fla., June 3, 2027 — Clearwater Plumbing technician Marcus Bell passed the Florida master plumber exam in May, making him the fifth licensed master plumber on the company's 22-person staff.

Why it works: it names the credential and who issued it, which a reader can check. Before announcing any award, make sure the issuer is real and that you are allowed to use its name. Pay-to-enter awards that every applicant wins are not news and editors know them on sight.

5. A partnership

Northside Food Pantry and Kettle Cafe Partner to Donate 500 Meals Monthly

COLUMBUS, Ohio, August 1, 2027 — Kettle Cafe will prepare 500 meals a month for Northside Food Pantry starting August 15, delivered every Friday to the pantry's Cleveland Avenue location.

Why it works: both parties named, the outcome counted, the schedule stated. A partnership release that only says two companies are "joining forces" has no news in it. Get written approval from the partner before you publish their name.

6. An event or sponsorship

Prairie Bike Works Hosts Free Kids' Bike Safety Day on September 12

LINCOLN, Neb., August 20, 2027 — Prairie Bike Works will host a free bike safety clinic for children ages 5 to 12 on Saturday, September 12, from 9 a.m. to noon at its O Street shop, with free helmet fittings while supplies last.

Why it works: date, time, place, audience and cost to attend, all in one sentence. Send event releases early enough for community calendars, and check each outlet's own submission deadline.

7. An anniversary or milestone

Lakeside Hardware Marks 50 Years in Downtown Madison

MADISON, Wis., October 4, 2027 — Lakeside Hardware, opened by Ellen Park in 1977 at 210 King Street, celebrates its 50th year on October 9 with the same family running the store and a staff of 14.

Why it works: a round number with a real story behind it. Milestones work best when they connect to something readers recognize: the founder, the building, the neighborhood.

8. A new offer or program

Mesa Home Repair Adds Free Safety Inspections for Homeowners 65 and Older

TUCSON, Ariz., January 12, 2027 — Mesa Home Repair will offer free one-hour home safety inspections to Tucson homeowners 65 and older through March 31, checking grab bars, stair rails, smoke detectors and entry lighting.

Why it works: a program with a public benefit and a defined end date. A plain discount ("20% off this month") is advertising, and most editors will treat it that way. It can still belong in your own newsroom and on your website, where your customers look for it.

Announcements that are rarely news

  • A redesigned website, unless it adds something customers can now do that they could not before.
  • A general statement of values or commitment with no event attached.
  • Reaching a follower count on social media.
  • A new logo on its own.

Not sure which side of the line your story falls on? Run it through the free newsworthiness check before you write a word.

Customer quotes and testimonials

If an example quotes a customer about your product, the quote has to reflect that person's honest experience, and any connection between you, such as a discount or payment, should be disclosed. The FTC explains the rules in its endorsement guides FAQ. When in doubt, quote someone from your own company instead.

Questions about examples

Can I copy these press release examples?

Yes. The companies and people are fictional, so copy the structure and replace every name, number, date and place with your own facts. Do not keep any detail you cannot verify.

What is the best type of press release for a small business?

The one tied to a real event with a local angle: a new location, new jobs, a new service, a credential, an event or a milestone. Each gives an editor a specific fact to report and a reason their readers would care.

How long should a small business press release be?

Usually 400 to 600 words. The examples here show only the headline and opening paragraph because those two pieces carry the news. The rest adds context, one quote, a short boilerplate and a media contact.

Is a sale or discount newsworthy?

Usually not on its own. Editors treat a plain discount as advertising. A program with a public benefit and a defined end date, such as free safety inspections for older homeowners, has a better chance of being treated as news.

Do I need permission to name a partner or award in a press release?

Get written approval from any partner before you publish their name, and confirm that an award or certification issuer allows you to reference it. Both protect you from a correction or a takedown request later.
