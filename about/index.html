About

We built this because we needed it

Distribute Press Releases is run by Eye To Ad Media, a Denver search agency working since 2012. We publish releases for our own companies and our clients on exactly the service sold here.

Why this exists

We run several businesses of our own alongside the agency, and we kept hitting the same wall. The cheap press release services published a page and produced a report nobody could check. The expensive ones were genuinely good and genuinely out of reach for a business publishing once a month.

What neither end offered was the thing that had actually changed: announcements are now read by crawlers, answer engines and automated agents as often as by people, and almost nothing being published was built for that. A single HTML page is a poor artifact for a world where half your readers want structured data.

So we built the pipeline we wanted. Fifteen artifacts instead of one link, every open channel run automatically, and a public receipt so the distribution report is something anyone can verify rather than something you have to take on faith.

How we behave

  • We say no. If your announcement will not hold up as news, we tell you before taking payment. We would rather lose $99 than publish something that was never going to work.
  • We label everything. Every release is marked as paid. Every link carries rel="sponsored". Releases from companies we operate say that we operate them.
  • We do not quote numbers we cannot show. No outlet counts, no reach figures, no borrowed media logos. If it is not on your receipt page with a working link, we do not claim it.
  • We do not promise outcomes. Rankings, pickup and AI citations belong to systems nobody here controls. We promise the pipeline runs and the artifacts ship, because those are checkable.
  • We publish the unflattering research. Our own page on press releases and AI visibility contains findings that argue against buying one. It is there because it is true.

Who is behind it

Eye To Ad Media has built search and web work since 2012 from Denver, Colorado. We also operate companies in home services and accessibility products, which means we are usually our own first customer for anything we build — this service included. When we say a tactic works or does not, it is generally because we tried it on our own money first.

Where a release we publish comes from a company we operate, that relationship is disclosed on the release itself.
