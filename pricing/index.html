Pricing questions

How much is a single press release?

$99, with writing, one editorial pass and all fifteen deliverables included. There are no per-word charges, no image or video fees and no industry-list upsells.

Is the annual plan actually cheaper?

Yes. Twelve releases cost $890 on the annual plan against $1,068 if you paid month to month, so you get two months free. It works out to roughly $74 per release.

Why is the monthly plan built around one release a month?

Because visibility in answer engines is rebuilt on a roughly four-week cycle rather than held permanently, so a steady publishing rhythm tends to do more than a single large send. One a month is the smallest rhythm that behaves like a rhythm.

What does the agency block include?

Twenty-five releases for $1,495, white-labeled so you can fold them into your own retainers, with credits usable across as many client companies as you like. It is a conversation rather than a checkout button.

Are there any hidden fees?

No. The only additional charge in the whole service is $25 for a change you request after approving a final draft. Anything we got wrong is corrected free, always.

How do I pay?

We invoice directly at the moment. Send your details through the order form and you will get an invoice with payment instructions. Nothing is charged until you have approved the scope.

Can I get a refund?

Before publication, yes, in full. Once a release is live it sits on permanent public URLs and has already gone into feeds, so it cannot be retroactively unpublished. That is exactly why there is an approval step before anything goes out.
