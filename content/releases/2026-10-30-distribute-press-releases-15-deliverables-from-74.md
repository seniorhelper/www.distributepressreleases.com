Distribute Press Releases, the Denver press release service run by Eye To Ad Media, now prices a complete release at $99, with the same 15 deliverables in every release and no add-on menu. Five releases cost $399, about $80 each. A monthly plan is $89 for one release a month, and an annual plan of 12 releases is $890, about $74 each, which works out to two months free.

Most press release services quote a low base price and then charge separately for extra words, images, wider distribution or search features. Distribute Press Releases builds those into one price. The client sends the facts, and the service writes the release and runs one editorial pass. Nothing publishes until the client approves the final draft, and approved releases go live within one to two business days of the facts.

Each release ships with a permanent canonical URL; NewsArticle and Organization structured data validated before publishing; a claims file that lists each fact as a dated, attributed statement that search engines and AI systems can lift; a clean markdown copy; a narrated MP3 the client keeps; a podcast episode with transcript; RSS, Atom, JSON Feed and Media RSS feeds filtered by industry and region; an IndexNow push within minutes of publishing; automatic posts to Bluesky and Mastodon; republication on a news property with correct canonical handling; relevance-matched placement on local network sites; and a free newsroom page the client can show on its own website. Every paid release is labeled as an announcement, and releases from businesses the operator runs carry that disclosure.

> Small businesses kept telling us the quote they got was never the price they paid. So we put everything we do into every release, at one number, and you approve the words before anything goes live.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Eye To Ad Media.

## How to order

Releases, 5-packs and plans can be bought at [distributepressreleases.com/pricing](https://distributepressreleases.com/pricing/), or a business can submit its release first and pay after approving the draft. Agencies can buy 25 white-labeled releases for $1,495 to use across client companies. Questions go to 1-800-481-8638.

## About Distribute Press Releases

Distribute Press Releases writes, publishes and syndicates press releases for small and midsize businesses, built for both traditional search and AI search. It is operated by Eye To Ad Media, a marketing, web development and software firm founded in 2012 and headquartered at 1001 Bannock St in Denver, Colorado.
