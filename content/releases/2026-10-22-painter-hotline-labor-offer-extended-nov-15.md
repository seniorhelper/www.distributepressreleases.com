Painter Hotline, the Colorado painting company at [painterhotline.com](https://painterhotline.com/), has extended its fall offer. Painting projects booked by Nov. 15, 2026, now receive 25% off labor. The discount covers labor only; paint and materials are not included. The offer had previously been advertised through Oct. 31.

Along with the extension, every estimate request on the company's site now asks the customer to choose how the estimate is done. A virtual estimate needs four questions answered and a few photos uploaded, and the written number usually comes back within one business day, with no appointment. Customers who would rather have someone look at the job can pick an in-person walkthrough instead. Both options are free.

The offer covers the work of the company's separate crews: interiors, exteriors, kitchen cabinet refinishing, commercial painting, deck and fence staining, and specialty coatings. Painter Hotline works across the Denver metro and Colorado's Front Range, from Castle Rock to Longmont and from Fort Morgan to Breckenridge.

> Projects booked by November 15 get 25% off labor. Customers can send photos for a written estimate, or have us come out and walk the job. Both estimates are free.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Search Converts LLC, which operates Painter Hotline.

## How to book

Homeowners and property managers can request either kind of estimate through the form at [painterhotline.com](https://painterhotline.com/) or call 720-208-5645. To receive the discount, the project has to be booked by Nov. 15, 2026.

## About Painter Hotline

Painter Hotline is a Colorado painting company with separate crews for interior, exterior, kitchen cabinet, commercial, deck and fence, and specialty coating work. It serves the Denver metro and the Front Range. Painter Hotline is operated by Search Converts LLC of Denver, which shares ownership with Eye To Ad Media, the operator of Distribute Press Releases.
