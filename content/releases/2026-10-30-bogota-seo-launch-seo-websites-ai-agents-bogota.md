Bogotá SEO has opened at bogotaseo.com to help businesses in Bogotá and across Colombia get found on Google, Google Maps and AI assistants, turn website visits into WhatsApp conversations, and hand repetitive work to AI agents. The agency serves clients in Colombian Spanish, mostly over WhatsApp, and starts every engagement with a free SEO audit.

Bogotá has no shortage of good businesses. According to the Bogotá Chamber of Commerce, the Bogotá region had 496,686 active companies in 2025, and 81,372 new ones were created between January and November of that year. Bogotá SEO's pitch is that quality alone no longer gets a business found: the bakery with the best pan de bono in the neighborhood still loses the customer if a competitor shows up first on Google Maps or answers WhatsApp faster.

The agency works in four steps it calls found, impress, convert and free time. It handles technical SEO and content written in Colombian Spanish, local SEO for neighborhoods such as Usaquén, Chapinero, Suba and Kennedy, and Google Business Profile setup, verification and weekly posting. It builds fast, mobile-first websites with WhatsApp one tap away, runs Google, Facebook and Instagram ads, and sets up AI agents that answer WhatsApp messages day or night, send a daily summary, follow up on quotes and ask happy customers for reviews, with a person in charge of anything important. The site also offers free tools, including a visibility check and a calculator for customers lost to unanswered WhatsApp messages, plus Spanish-language guides to Google Business Profile, Google Maps and WhatsApp Business chatbots.

The agency is run by Stephany, who was born in Colombia, spent her childhood in Bogotá and moved to the United States in her mid-teens. Her family still lives there, and her goal is to give the businesses of her hometown the same advertising and AI tools used by the most advanced companies in the world. Behind her is the Eye To Ad Media team in Denver, which has built websites, SEO and marketing since 2012, holds a 5.0 average across 60 Google reviews and is accredited A+ by the Better Business Bureau.

> Every tactic we sell gets tested on our own businesses first. Stephany knows how business actually gets done in Bogotá, and now the shops and service companies there get the same tools we use in Denver, in their own Spanish and on WhatsApp, where their customers already are.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Eye To Ad Media.

## How to start

Business owners can request a free quote or a free SEO audit at [bogotaseo.com](https://www.bogotaseo.com/), or message the team on WhatsApp from any page of the site. Bogotá SEO does not promise first place on Google; it explains what it can do, how results are measured and how far ahead the competition is.

## About Bogotá SEO

Bogotá SEO is a digital marketing agency for businesses in Bogotá and throughout Colombia, offering SEO, Google Maps and Google Business Profile management, websites and online stores, Google and Meta advertising, and AI agents for WhatsApp and back-office work. It is operated by Eye To Ad Media, a marketing, web development and software firm founded in 2012 and headquartered in Denver, Colorado.
