allofus.one, the free social network built in Denver, is inviting people to try a social platform designed around one idea: the member is in control. At [allofus.one](https://allofus.one/), members decide whose posts they see, whether they see sponsored posts at all, and what happens to their data. The platform has been in public beta since Oct. 11 and is free to join.

Most large social networks decide what each person sees with a ranking algorithm, and the settings that change that are often hard to find. allofus.one starts from the other direction. Its feed shows posts from the people and groups a member chose, newest first, with nothing ranked or inserted by an algorithm. Sponsored posts are labeled, and members can switch them off in their ad settings. The Control panel lets any member download their data or delete the account at any time.

## What members control

- **The feed.** Connections, groups and Brand pages a member follows, in the order they posted.
- **Ads.** Labeled sponsored posts, with an on-off switch in each member's ad settings.
- **Data.** Download it or delete the account from the Control panel.
- **Privacy.** The LIFEboard planner, with goals, habits, a journal, a focus timer, reminders and money tools, is private to the member.
- **How to show up.** One account works three ways: a regular feed, a 3D world in a phone or laptop browser, and the same world in virtual reality on a Meta Quest headset, with nothing to install.

## Free tools, family friendly

Members also get free video calls through Unity Call, groups, events, direct messages and Brand pages that let a small business run a page from a personal profile. The platform is family friendly. Accounts are for ages 13 and up, there is a language filter and report and block tools, and friend scan and voice chat keep teens with teens and adults with adults.

> Social media should work for the person using it. On allofus.one you pick who you follow, you see what they post in the order they posted it, and you decide whether you see ads. That's the whole idea, and it's free.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Eye To Ad Media, which operates allofus.one.

## Try it

allofus.one is free and open now at [allofus.one](https://allofus.one/). Open allofus.one/world/ in the browser on a Quest headset to try the VR mode. Bug reports can be sent from the report option in the site menu.
