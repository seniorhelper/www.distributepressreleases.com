Distribute Press Releases, the press release service operated by Denver-based Eye To Ad Media, now sends every release it publishes through a routing network. Each release is republished on the news site Action Global News with a canonical link back to the original. A filter then reads the release's industry, location and wording and places it on up to three sites where it fits. The network launches with five sites covering Colorado camping, Denver restaurants, Denver things to do, Colorado community news and Hawaii hotels.

The filter's rules are public. A Denver restaurant opening goes to the Denver restaurant site, a camping gear launch goes to the camping site, and a Honolulu hotel announcement goes to the Hawaii site. Local sites only take local releases. Every placement keeps the original's paid or affiliated label, and every release gets a public receipt listing where it went, with a working link for each destination.

> Most small companies pay for a press release and never find out where it went. We built this so every release lands somewhere it belongs, and the receipt shows you exactly where. If a release doesn't fit a site, it doesn't go there.

Zach Wennstedt, founder of Eye To Ad Media.

## What a release includes

Each release is published at a permanent address with news structured data, a markdown copy, a claims file and narrated audio when available. It appears in RSS, Atom, JSON Feed and Media RSS feeds filtered by industry and region, and in a company newsroom with an embed for the company's own website. New pages are submitted to IndexNow-participating search engines once they are live. The service does not promise placement on outlets it does not control and does not sell followed links. Links in every release are marked sponsored.

A single release is $99 and includes writing and one editorial pass. Monthly, annual and agency plans are listed at [distributepressreleases.com/pricing](https://distributepressreleases.com/pricing/). A free release builder at [distributepressreleases.com/tools/release-builder](https://distributepressreleases.com/tools/release-builder/) shows where a draft would be routed before it is sent.

## About Eye To Ad Media

Eye To Ad Media is a Denver marketing and web development firm founded in 2012. It operates Distribute Press Releases and a portfolio of local information sites, and builds websites, search programs and custom software for small and mid-sized businesses.
